01 Our Story

A cliff-top haven above the Arabian Sea

Aurelia Hotel sits on the edge of Varkala's ancient red laterite cliffs, 30 metres above the turquoise waters of the Arabian Sea. Here, the air smells of sea salt, coconut groves, and cardamom, and the horizon belongs purely to you.

Designed with native Keralan craftsmanship — exposed laterite stone, hand-turned teak timber, and breathable linen textiles — every space is conceived as a quiet, unhurried conversation between the cliff and the ocean below.

04 Culinary Coast

Eat with the sea below you

Celebrating the rich Malabar coastline and fresh ocean catch.

Our kitchen honours the time-honoured flavours of southern Kerala — fresh catch from the morning catamaran boats, coconut curries gently stewed with tamarind, and cold-climate wines chosen to complement the ocean breeze.

07 The Destination

Perched in Varkala, Kerala

Where the Arabian Sea kisses the ancient geological cliffs.

Located along the cliff-top promenade in Varkala, Aurelia Hotel offers seclusion while remaining within walking distance of local artisan cafés, Tibetan handicraft stalls, and peaceful beaches.
